## I don't know what you need — yet. But I can find out. {#i-dont-know}
That's how I start every project. I talk to the people who will use it and watch them try it. Then I test ideas with them until the results are clear.

## Hi, I'm Riccardo {#hi-im-riccardo}
I'm a UX researcher and designer finishing the EIT Digital master's in Human-Computer Interaction and Design. I've studied in Italy, Spain and France, and I work in Italian, English and Spanish.

I'm looking for a **UX research and design internship in Madrid, February to July 2027**, alongside my master's thesis.

## How I design {#how-i-design}
1. I find out before I design. I interview and observe people first, so the design follows what they actually do.
2. I test early, on paper or in Figma, and count what happens: mistakes, time on task and the SUS score.
3. I can build what I design, in front-end code or as a physical prototype with Arduino. I also look at the business model behind it.

## What makes me different {#what-makes-me-different}
- I've studied in Turin, Madrid and Trento, plus a summer school in France.
- I write code (HTML/CSS/JS, React, Angular) and build physical prototypes with Arduino and sensors.
- The EIT Digital programme taught me business models, go-to-market planning and pitching.

## What I'm curious about {#what-im-curious-about}
- AI and UX: how people make sense of AI tools and decide whether to trust them.
- Physical interfaces: interactive objects with sensors, and experiences that use more than one sense.

## Why baseball? {#why-baseball}
I play baseball on a team. It's a slow game of small decisions made together, and design feels similar to me. That's why this portfolio is a ballpark, with one project on each base.
