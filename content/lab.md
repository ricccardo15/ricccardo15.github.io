## Heuristic evaluation of a holiday booking site
*Individual · Evaluation of Interactive Systems, UPM · 2026*
I evaluated the full booking flow of a major theme-park travel site against Nielsen's heuristics and prioritised the problems: e.g. a "new search" link that wipes the whole session without confirmation, errors shown only through red borders (not accessible to colour-blind users) and the trip summary disappearing at checkout.
![A page of my heuristic evaluation report with an annotated screenshot of the booking form](/assets/lab/heuristic.webp)

## Discord for iOS vs Apple's guidelines
*Pair work with Andrea Ramaglia Bianchino · UX & Mobile Interaction, UPM · 2025*
We audited where Discord's iOS app departs from Apple's Human Interface Guidelines — hidden navigation instead of a tab bar, overloaded screens, weak visual hierarchy — and the impact on discoverability and cognitive load.
![Slide: Discord lacks a persistent iOS tab bar and hides navigation in a side drawer](/assets/lab/discord.webp)
