## I don't know what you need — yet. But I can find out.
That's how I start every project. I don't arrive with the answer: I go and get it, by talking to people, watching them use things and testing ideas until the evidence is clear.

## Hi, I'm Riccardo
I'm a UX researcher and designer finishing an EIT Digital master's in Human-Computer Interaction and Design. I've studied in three countries — Italy, Spain and France — and I work in Italian, English and Spanish.

I'm looking for a **UX research & design internship in Madrid, February–July 2027**, combined with my master's thesis.

## How I design
1. **Find out first.** Interviews and observation before opinions: I design for what people actually do.
2. **Test early, measure honestly.** On paper or in Figma, I put it in front of users and count mistakes, time and SUS.
3. **Make it real.** I can build what I design — front-end code or a physical prototype with Arduino — and I think about the business model behind it.

## What makes me different
- **International:** Turin, Madrid, Trento, and a summer school in France.
- **Technical:** HTML/CSS/JS, React, Angular, and physical prototyping with Arduino and sensors.
- **Business & innovation:** business models, go-to-market and pitching from the EIT Digital programme.

## What I'm curious about
- **AI and UX:** how people understand, use and trust AI systems.
- **Physical interfaces:** interactive objects, sensors and multisensory experiences.
